Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 7, 2026  |  Last Updated: April 7, 2026

Cybergrid Solutions LLC ("Cybergrid," "we," "us," or "our") operates the AEGIS Platform ("Platform"), a creator safety and digital intelligence system. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect information in connection with your use of the Platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Username and authentication credentials for Platform access
  • TikTok Data: Public TikTok user data accessed through TikTok's official APIs, including public usernames, follower counts, and public profile information
  • Evidence Data: Screenshots, timestamps, and metadata uploaded by operators for harassment documentation purposes
  • Honeypot Intelligence: IP addresses, user agents, geolocation data, and referrer information from visitors to intelligence collection pages deployed by Platform operators
  • Usage Data: Log data including access times, features used, and system events

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • Operating and maintaining the Platform
  • Enabling threat detection and harassment documentation
  • Generating evidence packages for law enforcement and platform trust and safety teams
  • Improving Platform functionality and security
  • Complying with legal obligations

3. TikTok API Data

AEGIS Platform accesses TikTok data through TikTok's official developer APIs. We access only public data that TikTok makes available through authorized API endpoints. We do not:

  • Store TikTok user passwords or authentication tokens belonging to third parties
  • Access private TikTok messages or non-public content
  • Use TikTok data for advertising or commercial profiling purposes
  • Share TikTok user data with third parties except as required by law

All TikTok API usage complies with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and Platform Policies.

4. Honeypot Intelligence Data

The Platform includes honeypot intelligence tools that collect IP addresses and device metadata from individuals who visit intelligence collection pages. This data is collected for the sole purpose of documenting coordinated harassment campaigns and supporting law enforcement investigations. This data:

  • Is stored securely and accessible only to authorized Platform operators
  • Is used exclusively for harassment documentation and law enforcement reporting
  • Is not sold, shared, or used for commercial purposes
  • May be disclosed to law enforcement agencies pursuant to valid legal process

5. Data Storage and Security

We store data using industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission via TLS/HTTPS
  • Authentication-protected API endpoints
  • Redis-based secure data storage with access controls
  • Google Cloud Platform infrastructure with enterprise security standards

6. Data Retention

We retain collected data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including supporting active law enforcement investigations. Evidence data is retained for a minimum of two years to support potential legal proceedings.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties. We may disclose data in the following circumstances:

  • To law enforcement agencies pursuant to valid legal process such as subpoenas, court orders, or search warrants
  • To social media platform trust and safety teams for harassment reporting purposes
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Cybergrid, our users, or the public
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with appropriate confidentiality protections

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access data we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • The right to object to processing of your data

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

9. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

11. California Privacy Rights

California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). For information about exercising your California privacy rights, please contact us using the information below.
